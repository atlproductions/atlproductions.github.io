Elements Slash - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

The game "Elements Slash" does not collect, store or share personal data itself. It has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. The only service that receives information about your game is Google Play Games, described below.

Your progress

Your progress and settings are saved only on your device, in the game's private storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and uninstalling the game deletes them.

Google Play Games

The game uses Google Play Games Services for achievements. When the game starts, it signs you in to Google Play Games automatically if you have a Play Games profile on your device. When you complete a quest, or make progress toward one, the game reports that achievement progress to Google so it appears in your Play Games profile.

This information is sent to Google and handled by Google, not by us. We never receive your name, email address or Google account details, and the game does not store your Play Games player ID. Google may process your Play Games profile, your achievements and related usage data under its own privacy policy. You can view your achievements, change your profile visibility or delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.

Play Games is optional: the game is fully playable without signing in, and without it nothing is sent.

Internet connection

To make sure the Daily Challenge changes on the right day, the game reads the current date from the internet. It sends a simple request to google.com, cloudflare.com or apple.com and only reads the time from the reply. No information about you or your game is sent. Like any website, these services can see standard connection details such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

  • Internet: for the date check described above and for Google Play Games achievements.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback in battle. You can turn it off in the game's Options.

Google Play

If you installed this game from Google Play, Google Play collects some data on its own. Please see Google's privacy policy here: Google Privacy Policy

For Google Play Games, see also: Google Play Games profile and privacy

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
