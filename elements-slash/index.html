Elements Slash - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

"Elements Slash" is made by ATL Productions, Italy, who is responsible for this game and this policy. The game has no accounts of its own and no ads. It uses three services, each described below: Google Play Games (achievements and cloud save, optional), Google Play (in-app purchases, optional) and Unity Analytics (gameplay statistics, only if you agree).

Your progress

Your progress and settings are saved on your device, in the game's private storage. Uninstalling the game deletes them. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is also saved to your Google account (see Cloud save below).

Google Play Games

The game uses Google Play Games Services for achievements and cloud save. When the game starts, it signs you in to Google Play Games automatically if you have a Play Games profile on your device.

Achievements: when you complete a quest, or make progress toward one, the game reports that achievement progress to Google so it appears in your Play Games profile.

Cloud save: while you are signed in, the game saves a copy of your progress (battles, items, upgrades and other game progress) to Google Play Games Saved Games in your Google account, so you can restore it on another device. The game never replaces the progress on your device without asking you first.

This information is sent to Google and handled by Google, not by us. We never receive your name, email address or Google account details, and the game does not store your Play Games player ID. Google may process your Play Games profile, your achievements, your saved games and related usage data under its own privacy policy. You can view your achievements, change your profile visibility or delete your Play Games data, saved games included, in the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.

Play Games is optional: the game is fully playable without signing in, and without it nothing is sent to Google Play Games.

In-app purchases

The game offers optional one-time packs. Purchases are made and paid through Google Play: we never see or store your payment details. The game receives from Google Play only the information needed to deliver what you bought (which product, and a purchase receipt that the game checks on your device), and records which packs were delivered in your save. If a purchase is refunded, the game removes what that pack gave. Google handles purchases under its own terms and privacy policy.

Unity Analytics (only with your consent)

The first time you open the game, it asks whether you want to share gameplay data. Nothing is sent unless you choose "Share", and you can change your choice at any time in the game's Options (Share gameplay data: On / Off). The game works the same either way.

If you agree, the game sends gameplay statistics to Unity Analytics, a service of Unity Technologies, to help us understand how the game is played and improve it:

  • battles started and how they ended (won, lost, quit or restarted), with the battle number, its duration, the wave reached, and how many times each spell and element was used;
  • which main screens of the camp you open;
  • opening the in-game Store, tapping a pack's buy button, and completed or refunded pack purchases (the pack's product name only, never payment details).

Unity also collects standard technical data with these events, such as a random identifier created for this installation, the device model, operating system, language, approximate country (derived from your IP address), game version and session times. We do not send your name, email address, Google account, location or any other personal details, and we do not use this data for advertising or sell it.

The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Options. Unity processes this data on our behalf under its own terms and privacy policy: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

Your rights

If you are in the European Union or a similar jurisdiction, you can ask to access or delete the data collected through Unity Analytics, or object to its use, by contacting us at the address below. Because we don't know who you are, please include the approximate date you played and your device model so we can find your data. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

Internet connection

The game is fully playable offline. It uses the internet only for Google Play Games, Google Play purchases and, if you agreed, Unity Analytics, as described above. Like any website, these services can see standard connection details such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: for Google Play Games, purchases and, with your consent, Unity Analytics.
  • Google Play billing: for the optional in-app purchases.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback in battle. You can turn it off in the game's Options.
