Elements Slash - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

The game "Elements Slash" does not collect, store or share personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

Your progress

Your progress and settings are saved only on your device, in the game's private storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and uninstalling the game deletes them.

Internet connection

To make sure the Daily Challenge changes on the right day, the game reads the current date from the internet. It sends a simple request to google.com, cloudflare.com or apple.com and only reads the time from the reply. No information about you or your game is sent. Like any website, these services can see standard connection details such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

  • Internet: only for the date check described above.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback in battle. You can turn it off in the game's Options.

Google Play

If you installed this game from Google Play, Google Play collects some data on its own. Please see the privacy policy for Google Play Services here: Google Play Privacy Policy

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
